## Student policy

1. Student Status Policy
Student statuses must be maintained by category, with independent statuses for each course enrollment.
Category	Statuses
Institution Status	Registered, Active, Deactivated, Suspended
Enrollment Status	Enrolled, Ongoing, On Hold, Completed, Discontinued, Cancelled
Academic Status	Pending, Ongoing, Incomplete, Failed, Completed
Examination Status	Scheduled, Appeared, Absent, Cancelled
Examination Result	Pending, Passed, Failed
Financial Status	Unpaid, Partial, Paid, Overdue
Certificate Status	Not Eligible, Pending, Processing, Ready, Issued, Delivered
Book Status	Not Issued, Issued, Returned, Lost, Damaged

Policy rules:
- Each student has one permanent profile.
- A student can enroll in multiple courses simultaneously or at different times.
- Each enrollment maintains its own independent academic, examination, financial, and certificate statuses.
- One enrollment's status must never affect another enrollment.
2. Institutional Status Control Policy
CMUD has the authority to activate, deactivate, or suspend any student.
- Active: Student can participate in institutional activities, subject to applicable policies.
- Deactivated: Student cannot participate in new institutional or academic activities until reactivated.
- Suspended: Student's participation is restricted due to disciplinary, administrative, or other approved reasons.
- Registered: Student profile exists, but admission is not yet confirmed.
Suspension and deactivation must preserve all existing academic, financial, and certificate records.
3. Semester Fee and Examination Policy
- Every semester has an applicable fee.
- A student must fully clear the applicable semester fee before participating in its examination.
- Unpaid or partially paid semester fees make the student ineligible for that examination.
- Approved discounts and fee waivers are considered when calculating payable fees.
- CMUD may grant an exceptional examination permission through an approved financial authorization.
4. Examination and Certificate Policy
- Students must complete all mandatory academic requirements and pass the required examinations to qualify for a course certificate.
- Passing one examination does not automatically mean the entire course is completed.
- Failed examinations may be retaken according to CMUD's examination policy.
- Certificate preparation, issuance, and delivery are separate activities.
- Certificate reissue must preserve the original certificate history.
5. Book Distribution Policy
- Each course enrollment allows a student to receive a maximum of one book.
- A student enrolled in multiple courses may receive one book for each enrollment.
- A second book cannot be issued for the same enrollment while the first book remains issued.
- Lost or damaged books may be replaced through an approved replacement process.
- All book distribution and replacement history must be maintained.
6. Independent Enrollment Policy
- Every course enrollment has its own academic progress, examination records, financial obligations, and certificate records.
- Completing or discontinuing one course does not affect another course.
- Financial dues for one enrollment must not automatically block academic activities in another enrollment.
